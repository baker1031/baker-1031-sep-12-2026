I keep seeing an offering’s fee load treated as a footnote once the exchange deadline is close. Cost deserves the same careful reading as the real estate and the tax mechanics.

First-order thinking stops at “no added fee.” Second-order thinking reads the total load, each compensation layer, ongoing fees, disposition economics, and the incentive structure before it judges the cost.

<main>
<a class="back-link" href="about.html">Back to About</a>

<section class="about-section" id="plain">
<div class="kicker">Plain English</div>
<h2 class="optima">Who pays, and how much.</h2>
<p>Investors do not pay Baker 1031 an advisory fee, a retainer, or an added commission. Our compensation is a selling commission paid by the sponsor out of each offering's disclosed cost load — the standard structure across the DST industry. Whether you invest through us or directly with a sponsor's wholesale desk, the offering's load is the same; working with an independent desk costs you nothing extra.</p>
<p>That structure also means our incentives deserve daylight, so here is the full picture of what a DST costs and where every dollar goes.</p>
</section>
<hr class="divider">
<section class="about-section" id="load">
<div class="kicker">The Total Load</div>
<h2 class="optima">What an offering&rsquo;s load includes</h2>
<div class="pillars" style="grid-template-columns:repeat(2,1fr);">
<div class="pillar"><h3>Selling Commissions</h3><p>Paid by the sponsor to the broker-dealer and the representative — this is where Baker 1031's compensation comes from. Disclosed line-by-line in the PPM.</p></div>
<div class="pillar"><h3>Dealer-Manager Fee</h3><p>Compensation to the managing broker-dealer coordinating the offering's distribution.</p></div>
<div class="pillar"><h3>Organization &amp; Offering Costs</h3><p>Legal, accounting, marketing, and administrative costs of creating and distributing the offering.</p></div>
<div class="pillar"><h3>Acquisition &amp; Financing Fees</h3><p>Sponsor fees for sourcing, underwriting, and closing the property, and for arranging any financing.</p></div>
</div>
<p style="margin-top:14px;">Together these make up the <strong>total load</strong> — the percentage of the offering that does not go into the real estate. It varies by offering and sponsor, it is stated in every private placement memorandum, and we publish it on every offering page on this site (see the &ldquo;Total Load&rdquo; line in each fact table). Ongoing asset-management fees and a disposition fee at sale are separate, and likewise disclosed in the PPM.</p>
</section>
<hr class="divider">
<section class="about-section" id="alignment">
<div class="kicker">Alignment</div>
<h2 class="optima">How the structure keeps us honest</h2>
<p>Because commissions are broadly similar across institutional DST offerings, we have no financial reason to steer you toward one sponsor over another — which is exactly how we want it. Our coverage is sponsor-agnostic, our diligence is published, and our analyst notes flag the cons alongside the pros on every offering.</p>
<p>Recommendations are made under Regulation Best Interest and reviewed by the supervising registered principal at Aurora Securities. Before you invest, we walk through the fee table in the PPM together, so the load, the ongoing fees, and the disposition economics are understood — not discovered.</p>
<p>Have a fee question we have not answered? <a href="contact.html" style="color:#005588;text-decoration:none;">Ask the desk directly</a>.</p>
