# The Real Estate Tax Center

I keep seeing real estate tax planning begin with a tactic instead of a tax bill. The useful order is to understand the exposure, then compare the legal tools that may change it.

First-order thinking searches for the largest deduction or deferral. Second-order thinking starts with the actual gain, recapture, surtax, state tax, timing, liquidity, and the limits of each strategy.

<div class="lintro">
<p class="lead">Real estate is taxed in layers — a capital-gains rate, a depreciation-recapture rate of up to 25%, a 3.8% net investment income surtax, and state tax — and it offers more legal ways to defer or erase those layers than almost any other asset. The Tax Center organizes both halves: first the exposure, then the tools to manage it.</p>
<p>Our <a href="insights.html" style="color:var(--blue-d);border-bottom:1px solid var(--blue)">Guides</a> are organized by strategy and life goal. The Tax Center is organized by <em>tax outcome</em> — start with what you owe, then move down to defer, eliminate, and shelter. Prefer to talk it through? <a href="contact.html" style="color:var(--blue-d);border-bottom:1px solid var(--blue)">Reach an advisor</a> anytime.</p>

<div class="wk">2026–27 Deadline Watch</div>
<h2>Dates and law changes worth tracking</h2>
<div class="wgrid">
<div class="wi"><div class="wd"><b>Dec 31, 2026</b> — Original OZ deferral ends</div><p>Capital gains deferred under the 2017 Opportunity Zone rules are recognized on your 2026 return (filed in 2027), regardless of when you invested.</p></div>
<div class="wi"><div class="wd"><b>Jan 1, 2027</b> — Opportunity Zones become permanent</div><p>OZ 2.0 begins: a permanent program with a rolling five-year deferral and a single 10% basis step-up (30% for qualifying rural funds).</p></div>
<div class="wi"><div class="wd"><b>Now permanent</b> — 100% bonus depreciation</div><p>The 2025 tax law restored full first-year bonus depreciation for property placed in service after Jan 19, 2025 — making cost segregation more valuable than it has been in years.</p></div>
<div class="wi"><div class="wd"><b>45 &amp; 180 days</b> — the 1031 clocks</div><p>From your sale closing, you have 45 days to identify and 180 days to close replacement property. These statutory deadlines are not extended for weekends or holidays.</p></div>
