# Jerry Baker Named to GlobeSt.'s 2026 CRE Aspiring Leaders List

[Back to All Insights](insights.html)

**Company News · Baker 1031 Research · Updated June 2026 · 4 min read**

I keep coming back to a simple point about recognition in this business: it is useful only if it directs attention back to the work. GlobeSt. has named Baker 1031 founder Jerry Baker to its 2026 list of rising leaders in commercial real estate. That is welcome news. It is also an occasion to be clear about what it does—and does not—say.

First-order thinking treats an industry award as proof that an investor should have confidence in a firm or an investment. Second-order thinking asks what the firm actually does, how it makes decisions, where risks sit, and whether the investor can see the incentives. Recognition cannot substitute for that work.

## About the recognition

GlobeSt. named [Gerald F. “Jerry” Baker III](jerry-baker-bio.html), founder and managing principal of Baker 1031 Investments, to [CRE's Aspiring Leaders of 2026](https://www.globest.com/2026/06/02/cres-aspiring-leaders-of-2026/). It is GlobeSt.'s annual recognition of commercial real estate professionals under age 40.

The program recognizes young professionals whose work has changed their companies or the broader commercial real estate industry. A panel of industry experts selected the 2026 honorees. GlobeSt. says the panel considered professional accomplishments, the impact of each honoree's work, service to the industry, and a commitment to ethical standards and excellence.

> “I'm honored to be included in this year's group. I started Baker 1031 because investors facing a 45-day deadline deserve clear information, serious underwriting and someone accountable on the other end of the phone. I'm proud of what we've built, but we are still early.”
>
> — Jerry Baker, Founder & Managing Principal

## Why this matters to Baker 1031

Jerry founded Baker 1031 on a straightforward premise: an investor should be able to see how a DST or other private real estate offering is underwritten, where the risks sit, and how the advisor is paid. Baker 1031 works across sponsors; it does not manufacture or exclusively represent its own investment product.

That distinction matters most when a decision is uncomfortable. The easy response is to focus on the headline, the sponsor name, or the distribution. The harder and more useful response is to examine the real estate, debt, fees, sponsor, business plan, and tax structure. That is where a risk-aware process begins.

Baker 1031 publishes its [track-record methodology](methodology.html) and maintains a [market and sponsor Data Center](data-center.html). The aim is not to make a private offering look certain. It is to make the relevant information visible enough for an investor to judge it.
