I keep seeing a short exchange clock make it tempting to accept whatever replacement option arrives first. An independent desk is useful only if its standards remain intact when the deadline starts to press.

First-order thinking treats a 1031 process as a search for something to buy before the deadline. Second-order thinking asks whether the options, debt match, diligence, and decision process are strong enough to serve the investor after the clock has stopped.

<section class="about-section" id="who" style="margin-top: 28px;">
<div class="kicker">Who We Are</div>
<h2 class="optima">A specialist desk, not a storefront.</h2>
<p>Most exchanges are won or lost on two things: the quality of the replacement options on the table, and the discipline to move before the 45- and 180-day clocks run out. We exist to give accredited investors both.</p>
<p>We are independent and sponsor-agnostic. We do not manufacture our own deals or push proprietary product. Our work is to understand an investor's exchange, surface the offerings that actually fit, and bring institutional-grade diligence to a decision that has to be made on a deadline.</p>
<p>We serve accredited investors only, because the strategies we work in — DSTs and private real-estate securities — are sold exclusively to them.</p>

<section class="about-section" id="heritage">
<div class="kicker">The Heritage</div>
<h2 class="optima">Three generations on the same side of the table.</h2>
<p>The name carries a history. Real estate has been the family's business across three generations — through cycles, downturns, and recoveries that taught a simple lesson: the deal matters, but the standard you hold it to matters more.</p>
<p>That inheritance shows up in how we work. We have sat on the ownership side of buildings, not just the brokerage side of trades, and we bring that operator's instinct to every sponsor and offering we evaluate. It is also why accountability is not a marketing word here — it is the family business.</p>

<div class="kicker">How We Work</div>
<h2 class="optima">From a ticking clock to a closed exchange.</h2>
<div class="steps">
<div class="step">
<div class="n">01</div>
<div>
<h3>Understand the Exchange</h3>
<p>We start with your relinquished property, timeline, debt, and objectives — the constraints that define what a suitable replacement even looks like.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="step">
<div class="n">02</div>
<div>
<h3>Curate the Options That Fit</h3>
<p>From offerings across the sponsors we cover, we shortlist those that match your dollar amount, debt replacement, asset preferences, and risk tolerance — including backups so a stalled deal does not fail.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="step">
<div class="n">03</div>
<div>
<h3>Bring the Diligence</h3>
<p>We pressure-test sponsor, structure, leverage, and business plan against a consistent standard, and walk you through the offering documents and the risks — not just the highlights.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="step">
<div class="n">04</div>
<div>
<h3>Execute on the Deadline</h3>
<p>We coordinate with your qualified intermediary and advisors to identify within 45 days and close within 180 — where discipline is everything.</p>
</div>

<div class="kicker">Common Questions</div>
<h2 class="optima">About Baker 1031</h2>
<div style="margin-top: 12px;">
<details class="faq-item">
<summary>What is Baker 1031 Investments?</summary>
<div class="answer">Baker 1031 Investments is an independent real-estate-securities brokerage in San Francisco that helps accredited investors complete 1031 exchanges using Delaware Statutory Trusts (DSTs), and also works in Qualified Opportunity Zone funds, 721 UPREIT exchanges, mineral and royalty interests, and REITs. Securities are offered through Aurora Securities, Inc., member FINRA/SIPC.</div>
</details>
<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Is Baker 1031 independent?</summary>
<div class="answer">Yes. Baker 1031 is independent and sponsor-agnostic — not owned by, and not exclusively representing, any single sponsor. We do not manufacture our own deals or push proprietary product; our coverage spans the institutional DST and 1031 universe, and our job is to find the right fit for the investor.</div>
</details>
<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Who can work with Baker 1031?</summary>
<div class="answer">We serve accredited investors only, because the strategies we work in — DSTs and private real-estate securities — are sold exclusively to them under SEC Rule 501 of Regulation D.</div>
</details>
<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Who is the broker-dealer, and how is Baker 1031 paid?</summary>
<div class="answer">Securities are offered through Aurora Securities, Inc. — member FINRA/SIPC. Compensation comes from selling commissions paid by the sponsor as part of each offering's disclosed load, not as an added fee to the investor; every fee is disclosed in the offering's private placement memorandum.</div>
</details>
