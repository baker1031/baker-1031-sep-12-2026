<p class="editorial-note">I keep seeing past-performance figures turn into a shorthand for certainty. First-order thinking stops at an average. Second-order thinking asks about the source, scope, timing, calculation, and what the figure leaves out.</p>

<p class="byline">By Jerry Baker &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; Reviewed by a registered principal &nbsp;&middot;&nbsp; Updated June 2026</p>
<div class="article-intro" style="background:#FCF7F0;border:1px solid #FCF7F0;padding:14px 18px;margin-top:14px;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>What our numbers mean — and what they don't.</strong> Every performance figure on this site carries an asterisk. This is where the asterisk leads. In short: figures are sponsor-reported, cover realized programs only, are net of fees and expenses, and are not a forecast or a recommendation.</p>
</div>

<section class="about-section" id="data">
<div class="kicker">The Data</div>
<h2 class="optima">Where the figures come from</h2>
<p><strong>Sources.</strong> Performance is compiled from sponsor-published full-cycle track records, offering materials, investor reporting, and public filings, and presented as reported by the sponsor. We do not audit, recalculate, or independently verify them.</p>
<p><strong>Scope.</strong> Aggregate statistics reflect full-cycle (realized) programs — offerings acquired, operated, and ultimately sold — so total investor proceeds are known. Active and unrealized programs are excluded until they complete.</p>
<p><strong>Coverage.</strong> Sponsor profiles span the institutional 1031 / DST universe we track; a subset are designated preferred sponsors, reflecting the depth of our diligence coverage. Figures are point-in-time, not continuously audited.</p>
</section>
<hr class="divider">
<section class="about-section" id="definitions">
<div class="kicker">Definitions</div>
<h2 class="optima">What each metric means</h2>
<dl class="glossary-list" style="margin:0;"><div class="glossary-term"><dt>Full-Cycle Program</dt><dd>An offering that has been raised, deployed, operated, and exited, so the total return to investors is known. Active programs are excluded from realized averages.</dd></div><div class="glossary-term"><dt>Average Annual Return</dt><dd>The average annualized total return across a sponsor's full-cycle programs, as the sponsor reports it (some IRR, some yield + appreciation), net of fees, sponsor load, and program expenses. We do not reconcile to a single basis.</dd></div><div class="glossary-term"><dt>Equity Multiple (&ldquo;x&rdquo;)</dt><dd>Total cash returned divided by equity invested. 1.8x means $1.00 returned $1.80 over the hold. A multiple ignores time — read it alongside hold period.</dd></div><div class="glossary-term"><dt>Hold Period</dt><dd>Time from acquisition to exit, in years, averaged across realized programs. The hold is set by the sponsor's business plan, not the investor.</dd></div><div class="glossary-term"><dt>Success Rate</dt><dd>The share of full-cycle programs that returned at least an investor's original equity, as reported by the sponsor. Definitions differ; it measures avoidance of loss, not the size of gains.</dd></div><div class="glossary-term"><dt>AUM</dt><dd>The sponsor's reported total assets under management — a measure of scale and tenure, not of DST-program performance, and may include unrelated strategies.</dd></div><div class="glossary-term"><dt>Mean vs. Median</dt><dd>Where we lead with a mean we show the median alongside it. When the two diverge, the spread is the story — averages conceal dispersion, and dispersion is risk.</dd></div></dl>
</section>
<hr class="divider">
<section class="about-section" id="aggregation">
<div class="kicker">Aggregation</div>
<h2 class="optima">Turning many programs into one number</h2>
<p>Aggregate statistics are computed across realized programs, generally equal-weighting each program unless stated as dollar-weighted. We report sponsor-universe and preferred-sponsor figures separately, and lead with the mean while disclosing the median. Because sponsors report on differing bases, treat these aggregates as directional context — not a precise, like-for-like benchmark, and never an expected return for any specific offering.</p>
</section>
<hr class="divider">
<section class="about-section" id="limitations">
<div class="kicker">Limitations</div>
<h2 class="optima">What these numbers are not</h2>
<div class="pillars" style="grid-template-columns:repeat(3,1fr);">
<div class="pillar"><h3>Survivorship</h3><p>Realized-only means a sponsor's currently active or troubled programs aren't yet in the average.</p></div>
<div class="pillar"><h3>Net — But Taxes Vary</h3><p>Net of commissions, sponsor load, and program expenses; your after-tax result depends on your circumstances.</p></div>
<div class="pillar"><h3>Not Normalized</h3><p>Different sponsors compute returns differently; cross-sponsor comparisons are imperfect.</p></div>
<div class="pillar"><h3>Dispersion</h3><p>An average is not a typical outcome; individual programs range widely around it.</p></div>
<div class="pillar"><h3>Unverified</h3><p>Performance is sponsor-reported and compiled from sponsor and public sources; we have not audited it.</p></div>
<div class="pillar"><h3>Not a Forecast</h3><p>Past full-cycle results do not indicate, predict, or guarantee any future offering.</p></div>
</div>
</section>
<hr class="divider">
<section class="about-section" id="preferred">
<div class="kicker">Preferred = Coverage, Not Endorsement</div>
<h2 class="optima">How a sponsor becomes &ldquo;preferred&rdquo;</h2>
<p>A preferred sponsor is one whose platform, programs, and reporting we cover in depth. The designation reflects the breadth of our diligence relationship, not a rating, ranking, endorsement, or assurance about outcomes. Coverage considers tenure and multi-cycle history, transparency of reporting, asset/strategy focus, and structural and sponsor diligence. A sponsor's absence from the list is not a negative judgment.</p>
</section>

<section class="about-section" id="mfaq">
<div class="kicker">Common Questions</div>
<h2 class="optima">Frequently asked</h2>
<div style="margin-top:12px;"><details class="faq-item"><summary>Are these returns net of fees and taxes?</summary><div class="answer">Net of selling and dealer-manager commissions, sponsor load, and ongoing program fees and expenses. Your individual tax result depends on your own circumstances, so we don't represent figures as net of your personal taxes.</div></details><details class="faq-item"><summary>Do the averages include deals that lost money?</summary><div class="answer">Yes — realized averages include every full-cycle program a sponsor reports, including those that returned less than invested equity. The separate success-rate figure shows the share of programs that returned at least original equity.</div></details><details class="faq-item"><summary>Has Baker independently verified the figures?</summary><div class="answer">No. Performance is sponsor-reported and compiled from sponsor and public sources; we do not audit, recalculate, or independently verify it.</div></details><details class="faq-item"><summary>Does &ldquo;preferred&rdquo; mean Baker recommends a sponsor?</summary><div class="answer">No. Preferred reflects the depth of our diligence coverage of a sponsor — not a rating, ranking, endorsement, or assurance about outcomes.</div></details></div>
</section>
