<p class="editorial-note">I keep seeing a 1031 question arrive only after a sale decision is already set. First-order thinking treats specialist execution as a handoff. Second-order thinking asks whether roles, deadlines, and suitability have been made clear while the client’s primary relationship remains intact.</p>

<section class="about-section" id="model">
<div class="kicker">The Partnership Model</div>
<h2 class="optima">You keep the client. We handle execution.</h2>
<p>When a client faces a taxable real estate sale, their financial advisor or CPA is the first call — and the 1031/DST answer is a specialist's job. That is where our desk fits. You retain the relationship, the planning role, and the client; we bring the sponsor coverage, the offering diligence, and the deadline execution.</p>
<p>We are independent and sponsor-agnostic, with 80+ sponsors under coverage and a published diligence methodology. We do not manage assets, we do not cross-sell, and we do not compete for your client's broader financial relationship. Our lane is the exchange.</p>
</section>
<hr class="divider">
<section class="about-section" id="engagement">
<div class="kicker">How It Works</div>
<h2 class="optima">A typical engagement</h2>
<div class="steps">
<div class="step"><div class="n">01</div><div><h3>Pre-Sale Planning</h3><p>Before the property closes, we model the exchange math with you — proceeds, debt replacement, boot exposure, and timing — so the client enters the 45-day window with a plan instead of a scramble.</p></div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="n">02</div><div><h3>Portfolio Construction</h3><p>We shortlist DST offerings that fit the client's dollar amount, debt-replacement requirement, income needs, and risk tolerance, with backups identified under the 45-day rules. You see everything we see, including our written analyst notes on each offering.</p></div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="n">03</div><div><h3>Suitability &amp; Subscription</h3><p>Accreditation and suitability run through Aurora Securities, the supervising broker-dealer. Documents are prepared for the client, and most DST allocations can close in two to three business days.</p></div></div>
<div class="step"><div class="n">04</div><div><h3>Through Full Cycle</h3><p>After closing we track each position, forward sponsor reporting, and coordinate the next event — a subsequent exchange, a 721 roll where offered, or a taxable exit — with you in the loop throughout.</p></div></div>
</div>
</section>
<hr class="divider">
<section class="about-section" id="compensation">
<div class="kicker">Compensation</div>
<h2 class="optima">Clean lines, fully disclosed</h2>
<p>Our compensation comes from the selling commissions sponsors pay as part of each offering's disclosed load — the client writes no separate check, and your advisory or preparation fees are unaffected. Where your firm's arrangements permit RIA participation or licensed splits, we structure that through the broker-dealer, in writing, before any transaction.</p>
<p>If a client situation is not a fit for a DST — a small gain, a need for liquidity, a better answer in an installment sale or simply paying the tax — we say so. The partnership only works if the advice holds up.</p>
</section>
