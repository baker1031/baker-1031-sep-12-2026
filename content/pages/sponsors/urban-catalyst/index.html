Urban Catalyst Overview

Urban Catalyst is a San Jose Opportunity Zone fund manager and developer, founded in 2017, concentrating on ground-up development in downtown San Jose—a deliberately tight geographic thesis tied to Silicon Valley growth. With Fund I closed at $131 million and Fund II advancing a diversified downtown development portfolio, the firm has earned external recognition, including a top-4% Novogradac OZ ranking, Forbes OZ honors and a 2024 Silicon Valley Developer of the Year award. For QOZ investors, it offers focused, well-regarded development exposure rather than stabilized cash flow.
