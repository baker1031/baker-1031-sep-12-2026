Blue Door Overview

Blue Door Asset Management I is a self-storage DST sponsor and a subsidiary of Strategic Storage Growth Trust III, an entity sponsored by an affiliate of SmartStop Self Storage REIT (NYSE: SMA). It packages SmartStop-operated self-storage facilities into Delaware Statutory Trusts for 1031-exchange investors, in both all-cash and leveraged structures. Across its trusts, Blue Door holds roughly 5,420 storage units and about 697,400 net rentable square feet, backed by SmartStop's national self-storage operating and management platform.

Blue Door Strategies & Advantages

  • Self-storage specialist backed by SmartStop's national operating and management platform (NYSE: SMA).
  • Offers both all-cash (no-mortgage) and leveraged DST structures.
  • Institutional sponsor lineage through Strategic Storage Growth Trust III and SmartStop.
