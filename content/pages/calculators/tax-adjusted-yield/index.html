Depreciation shelter defers tax rather than eliminating it: sheltered distributions reduce your basis, which generally increases recapture and gain when the investment ends (a subsequent 1031 or 721 transaction may defer it again). Shelter percentages are sponsor projections that change year to year. Not tax advice — model your situation with your CPA.

This calculator is a simplified educational illustration, not tax or investment advice. It uses the figures you enter and standard federal rates without your full tax picture (basis adjustments, carryforwards, AMT, state specifics, and filing details are not modeled). Actual results are determined by your tax returns as prepared by your CPA. Review the PPM for any investment you consider and confirm every figure with your CPA and attorney before acting.
