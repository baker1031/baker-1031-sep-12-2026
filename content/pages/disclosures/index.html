Nature of the investments

Interests in Delaware Statutory Trusts, 721 exchange (UPREIT) structures, real estate investment trusts, opportunity zone funds, real estate credit vehicles, and oil & gas royalty programs are speculative, illiquid private placements offered under exemptions from registration, generally to accredited investors only. They involve substantial risk, including complete loss of principal. There is no public market for these interests, distributions are not guaranteed and may stop, financing terms can change outcomes materially, and tax treatment depends on facts specific to each investor.

Offers are made only by PPM

Nothing on this website is an offer to sell or a solicitation of an offer to buy any security. Offers are made exclusively through each program's private placement memorandum, which contains the complete terms, risk factors, fees, and conflicts, and which controls over anything summarized on this site.
