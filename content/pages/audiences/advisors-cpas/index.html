Advisors & CPAs: 1031 Exchange FAQ

How do you work with a client's existing advisor?

Collaboratively. We coordinate with the advisor, CPA, attorney, and qualified intermediary, keep everyone informed, and focus on the securities execution while the advisor keeps the overall relationship.

What kinds of offerings can my clients access?

Vetted DST, 721 UPREIT, mineral royalty, and Opportunity Zone offerings from multiple sponsors, available to accredited investors and made only through each sponsor's private placement memorandum.

Are these suitable for all clients?

No. These are illiquid, accredited-investor offerings that involve risk, including loss of principal. Suitability depends on the client's objectives, and we document the process carefully.
