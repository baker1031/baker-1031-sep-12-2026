Sponsor Provided Materials

The sponsors whose offerings I follow publish a good deal of educational material — exchange mechanics, trust structures, opportunity zone rules, sector research. This is a catalogue of what I keep on hand, so you can see what exists and ask for anything useful.

Every document here was written and published by the sponsor named on it. Baker 1031 did not write them, and the one-line summaries below are mine, not theirs. The documents themselves are sent on request rather than posted, so that each one reaches you in the form its author approved, and so I know what you have.

Nothing in this catalogue is an offer to sell or a solicitation of an offer to buy any security, and no document here is a recommendation of any sponsor or program. Sponsor material reflects that sponsor's own views and data, which Baker 1031 has not independently verified. Offerings are made only by private placement memorandum.
