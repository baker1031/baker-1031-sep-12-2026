NLC Financial Service HQ DST

Office · Single Tenant · Net Lease · Michigan

Overview

The Trust sells 79.30% of the interests in a Detroit, MI, 20-story, 421,481-sf office tower and 319-space parking garage. The property is 100% triple-net leased to The Huntington National Bank through June 2044, with four seven-year extension options, and the lease is guaranteed by Huntington Bancshares Incorporated. The offering allocates $113.94 million of the property's loan debt to offered interests, consisting of a senior note and two subordinated notes. Trust Agreement Section 9.3 permits the Trustee to contribute the property to a partnership under Code Section 721 at any time after the third anniversary; if the Trustee elects that path, each Beneficial Owner is offered the choice of selling their interest for cash before the contribution or participating in it.

Key highlights

  • 100% NNN lease to The Huntington National Bank through June 2044 with four seven-year extension options.
  • Huntington Bancshares Incorporated guarantees tenant obligations.
  • 421,481-sf Detroit office tower plus 319-space structured parking garage.
  • Loan maturities are coterminous with lease expiration, reducing intermediate refinance risk but leaving 2044 terminal-value concentration.
  • Offered interests carry $113.94 million of allocated loan debt versus $23.75 million of maximum cash equity.

Projected distributions · No Operating Cash Flow

This is a Zero Cash Flow offering. The Executive Summary and Brochure both state, under Offering Details: "OFFERING TYPE: Zero Cash Flow, 100% of Lease rents paid to lenders." The PPM contains no annual cash-on-cash schedule, pro forma, or distribution projection of any kind — not because the schedule is undisclosed, but because none is projected to exist during the loan term, as all lease rent services the senior and subordinated notes. Investor return depends on residual value at the June 10, 2044 maturity; the Frisco Valuation Group appraisal values the fee interest at $175,000,000 as of March 1, 2026 and estimates a terminal value of $240,000,000 at expiration of the initial lease term.

Projections from the sponsor’s offering documents. Distributions are not guaranteed and may be lower than shown or suspended. See the PPM for assumptions.

Jerry’s notes on this offering

These are my opinions after reviewing the offering. They aren’t investment advice or a recommendation for any particular investor, and they don’t replace the PPM — please read it before investing.

This is a long-duration net-lease credit investment rather than an office operating-value-add case. Cash flow depends on Huntington rent and the enforceability of Huntington Bancshares' guaranty; the tenant, not the DST, handles operating obligations under the NNN lease. The offered interests have an 82.75% debt-to-transaction-capital ratio, driven by a 4.589% senior loan and 7.870% and 10.756% subordinated notes. Maturities align with the June 2044 lease end, limiting interim refinancing risk but concentrating terminal value, tenant-renewal and residual office-market risk in one date.

Structure note: this is a Zero Cash Flow deal — 100% of lease rent goes to the lenders and there are no interim distributions, so the entire return is residual. Interest Rate is recorded as the 4.589% senior coupon, which carries roughly 92% of principal; the subordinated coupons are 7.870% and 10.756%. Loan Term is recorded as 18 years, the term of the subordinated notes originated March 11, 2026; the senior note originated August 28, 2019 and runs approximately 25 years. All three mature June 10, 2044.
