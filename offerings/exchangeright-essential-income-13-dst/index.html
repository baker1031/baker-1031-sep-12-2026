ExchangeRight Essential Income 13 DST

Industrial · Distribution · Warehouse · Single Tenant · Net Lease · Minnesota

Overview

ExchangeRight Essential Income 13 DST holds a single approximately 753,640-square-foot industrial building on roughly 71.74 acres at 9800 217th Street West, Lakeville, Minnesota. The building was constructed in 2021 and is 100% leased to Amazon.com Services LLC as an Amazon Distribution Center, guaranteed by Amazon.com, Inc., which carries a long-term S&P rating of AA. The lease runs from August 9, 2021 to August 31, 2036 on a double net (NN) basis, with 1.5% annual rent escalations, fair market rent resets at each option, and four five-year renewal options. Zoning is I-1 Light Industrial.

The Trust acquired the property from an unaffiliated third party below its appraised value and is capitalized entirely with $107,600,000 of owner equity; there is no Trust-level debt. The stated exit is a tax-deferred Section 721 exchange into the operating partnership of the ExchangeRight Essential Income REIT, which cannot occur before the second anniversary of the Offering Termination Date. A July 31, 2026 appraisal by Partners Valuation Advisory supports that valuation, but it was prepared for an unaffiliated third party and expressly may not be relied upon by the Trust or the Owners.

Key highlights

  • 753,640-square-foot, 2021-built Amazon distribution center on 71.74 acres in Lakeville, Minnesota, 100% leased with the lease guaranteed by Amazon.com, Inc. (S&P AA).
  • All-cash capitalization with no Trust-level debt eliminates refinancing, debt-service and interest-rate exposure.
  • Single asset, single tenant, and a double net (NN) rather than absolute net lease — the Trust retains landlord obligations that the sponsor's triple-net retail portfolios do not carry. The REIT-level diversification cited throughout the marketing (436 properties, 38 states, 44 tenants) belongs to the exchange vehicle, not to this Trust.
  • Lease runs to August 31, 2036 with 1.5% annual escalations and four five-year options; the July 2026 appraisal was prepared for an unaffiliated third party and expressly may not be relied upon by the Trust or the Owners.
  • 721 exchange into the ExchangeRight Essential Income REIT operating partnership is the stated exit and cannot occur before the second anniversary of the Offering Termination Date; the two-year cash flow presentation is sized to that event.

Jerry’s notes on this offering

These are my opinions after reviewing the offering. They aren’t investment advice or a recommendation for any particular investor, and they don’t replace the PPM — please read it before investing.

The tenant credit is about as strong as net lease offers: Amazon.com, Inc. at S&P AA on a 2021-built distribution center with ten years of remaining term and four five-year options behind it. All cash, no leverage.

Three features separate this from ExchangeRight's usual product. It is a single building with a single tenant. The marketing leads with the REIT's 436 properties across 38 states and 44 tenants, but that diversification belongs to the vehicle investors exchange into, not to what they own during the hold. It is a double net lease rather than the absolute NNN structure the sponsor's retail portfolios use, so the Trust retains landlord obligations. And year-one income is partly supported rather than earned: a $264,995 seller rent credit lifts contractual rent to $482,575.85 per month from closing through August 31, 2029.

This is the truncated-721 case, where the schedule stops at the anticipated roll-up by design. The material question is not the two years of cash flow but whether the exchange into the Essential Income REIT occurs on the terms, timing and valuation described. The appraisal offers no help there: the July 31, 2026 Partners Valuation Advisory report was prepared for an unaffiliated third party and expressly may not be relied upon by the Trust or the Owners.
