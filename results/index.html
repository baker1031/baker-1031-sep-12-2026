Asset classes here are the normalised classes held in the underlying dataset, not the raw property-type wording each sponsor used in its own offering documents — so every net-leased variant reports as net lease, and credit programs, which are loan positions secured by a property rather than ownership of it, report as one class rather than being mixed into the sectors they lent against. A few programs span two classes and are counted in both, so the counts add to more than the number of programs. Searching the full list below still matches a sponsor's own wording. A class with fewer than five completed programs shows its count and no averages: over one or two programs an average describes those programs, not the class.
